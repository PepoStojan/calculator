## Page recipe that tends to rank for calculators

1. The tool above the fold, results updating live, no signup.
2. A clear formula and a worked example (already templated in every page).
3. 3–5 real FAQs that match People-Also-Ask questions.
4. Internal links to related calculators (built in) and category hubs.
5. Fast pages (Core Web Vitals): these pages are static HTML, one small CSS file and one small JS file.
6. Earn links: embeddable widgets, a data-driven study (e.g. "cost of X"), outreach to teachers/finance bloggers.

## Realistic expectations

A new domain usually needs several months before long-tail pages rank, and backlinks matter. The top results for many head terms are Calculator.net, Omni, NerdWallet, Bankrate and similar. Plan for months of publishing and promoting. Traffic numbers in this file are potential, not promises.
